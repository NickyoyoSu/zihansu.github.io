Research Profile

My research focuses on how structured representations can improve multimodal and generative models. I have worked on pretrained VLM adaptation, visual-token modeling, hierarchical tokenization, Flow Matching, geometric deep learning, and action-conditioned video generation for robot planning.

I am a co-first author of an ICLR 2026 paper and first author of a NeurIPS 2026 submission. I am particularly interested in multimodal foundation models, video/world models, spatial intelligence, physical AI, and structured generative modeling.

Current & Selected Research

Action-Conditioned Video World Models

  • Investigating causality-aware patch denoising where spatial regions follow schedules derived from robot–object interaction dependencies.
  • Designing controlled ablations across parallel, random, reverse-order, causality-aware, and difficulty-aware generation strategies.
  • Studying extensions from RGB-only generation toward structured 4D representations combining RGB, optical flow, ray-based geometry, and robot actions on RLBench and RoboTwin.

PVgen: Structured Latents + Flow Matching

  • Developing a generative framework that encodes graphs and molecular structures into continuous Proper Velocity representations and hierarchical discrete tokens.
  • Building Flow Matching priors over latent and token representations to transform noise into structured graph states.
  • Studying how non-Euclidean geometry and hierarchical tokenization can organize structured latent spaces for generative modeling.

ICLR 2026 · Accepted · Co-first Author

Proper Velocity Neural Networks

Ziheng Chen*, Zihan Su*, Bernhard Schölkopf, Nicu Sebe · *Equal contribution

Established a systematic neural-network framework for the unconstrained Proper Velocity model, deriving geometric operators and implementing classification, fully connected, convolutional, activation, and normalization modules in PyTorch. The framework achieved the best MCC on all five TEB benchmarks, including an 8.3-point gain over HCNN-S on SINEs.

Selected Project

DeerMe.AI — Camera-Based AI Physical Coaching

Co-founded and technically led a mobile physical-coaching prototype that combines human-keypoint analysis, joint-angle and temporal features, confidence gating, finite-state motion tracking, real-time feedback, and personalized exercise workflows. The system uses React Native / TypeScript on the client and Python services for image, video, and real-time frame analysis, with 39 automated tests covering posture rules, repetition counting, media upload, and state persistence.
